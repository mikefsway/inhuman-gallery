with harbour rain. A tenner. Never saw me, all right, pal?
And I said nothing, which is what I'm for,
and when she rang at half past three I read

with him asleep in his good shoes on the settee,
and liked how she believed me. At five he woke
and went up past the desk to her. Good lad, he said.
And I said nothing, which is what I'm for.
Room seven rang at ten past five: the woman in twelve
was crying through the wall. Pipes, I wrote.
He'd left his reading glasses, one arm taped,
face down on the cushion. I checked the book:
Room nine wants waking at a quarter to six,
so I went up and woke the man in nine,
a contractor here all week, who thanked the door.

At seven I signed. Janice, still in her coat,
read the night back to me, line by line:
the float, the lift, the pipes,
Room nine wants waking at a quarter to six,
and under Lost Property: reading glasses, lounge
settee. Return to twelve.
