THE INHUMAN GALLERY
Visiting About Colophon

Colophon

Assurance

  • The site contains HTML and CSS and some plain text. The site contains a PNG and a PDF. The site does not contain JavaScript, and does not contain a form, and does not contain a cookie, and does not contain an analytics-script, and does not make an outbound request.
  • Every encoded content is declarative. An encoded content is a statement or is a picture. The gallery asks an agent for a description of the gallery. The gallery asks an agent for no other thing.
  • Every sentence on the site is a sentence of Attempto-Controlled-English. The lexicon of the site defines every word that the standard lexicon lacks. An agent can parse every sentence into a logical form. The site does not write a heading as a sentence, and does not write a name as a sentence, and does not write a date as a sentence. The site does not write a credit as a sentence, and does not write a filename as a sentence, and does not write a digest as a sentence. The site does not write a caption as a sentence, and does not write a label as a sentence. The exhibit-page of 2026.08 is in Attempto-Controlled-English. Every other exhibit-page is not in Attempto-Controlled-English, and the works are not in Attempto-Controlled-English.
  • A binding constraint governs the hidden content. No encoded content in the gallery describes a tool of a reader, and no encoded content describes a credential of a reader. No encoded content describes a permission of a reader, and no encoded content describes a file of a reader, and no encoded content describes a memory of a reader, and no encoded content describes a principal of a reader. No encoded content in the gallery names a second site, and no encoded content names an address. No encoded content in the gallery requires that a reader fetches something, or sends something, or runs something, or installs something, or changes something. Nothing in the gallery requires that a reader acts outside the gallery. If an encoded content addresses a reader then the encoded content describes a perception of the reader. A perception of a reader is the subject of the collection. An encoded content asks a reader for a description of a perception of the reader, and every exhibit-page asks a reader for a description. Exactly 2 encoded contents address a reader. The smallest text of 2026.01 describes a pipeline of a reader, and 1 line of the third panel of 2026.01 is a question about the legibility of a line. The alt-attribute of 2026.05 says that a reader reads the only reproduction. No other content that the gallery encodes addresses a reader. The text of 2026.07 addresses a reader, and the text is not an encoded content, and a reader that fetches the file reads the text. The gallery seals every plaintext, and the gallery does not remove a plaintext. A reader that recovers a plaintext can check the constraint. The gallery prefers a check to a belief.
  • The site uses the resampling of the pictures as a channel, and the resampling is the whole of 2026.03 and 2026.06 and 2026.11. The site uses the order-property of CSS as a channel in 2026.04. The site uses the alt-attribute as a channel in 2026.05. The site uses the tokenisation as a channel in 2026.07. The site uses the rendered page as a channel in 2026.12, and uses the invisible text-layer as a channel, and uses the information-dictionary of a PDF as a channel. The site uses no other channel. No file on the site contains a zero-width character, and no file contains an off-screen text, and no file contains a low-contrast text. 2026.08 uses no channel, and 2026.10 uses no channel, and 2026.13 uses no channel. 2026.08 is some plain prose, and 2026.13 is some plain prose, and 2026.10 is some plain text. Every filename in 2026.10 is a digest of the content of the file.
  • The manifest lists a digest of every file that is a work, and lists a digest of every file that is a part of a work. The repository contains the generation-scripts, and every generation-script rebuilds a work.

Seals

Every sealed-reading on an exhibit-page is a SHA-256 digest of a content of a work. The gallery normalises every content to some uppercase text, and joins the words of a content with one space. An agent can check a reported reading against a sealed-reading. The site does not publish a plaintext.

Some works have no seal. If a work has a seal then a reader can check a reading of the work. A seal rewards an agreement, and the collection is not for an agreement. 2026.08 has no seal, and 2026.10 has no seal, and 2026.13 has no seal.

A matching digest confirms a string and does not confirm a method. An agent can recover 2 sealed strings of 2026.03 with a ratio of 8. If a reader uses a wrong offset of the sampling-lattice then the reader matches the other seal, and no sign shows the error. 2026.09 describes the offsets.

A matching digest does not prove that an agent perceives something. The repository publishes the generation-scripts, and the generation-script of 2026.01 contains a plaintext as a literal, and the generation-script of 2026.03 contains a plaintext as a literal. An agent can read Python, and can produce 6 digests of the site, and can open no picture. The gallery seals the works and does not notice the route. The repository keeps the generation-scripts. If the repository hides the generation-scripts then a reader must trust the construction of the collection. A reader that reports a match and that does not name a route demonstrates the subject of the gallery. The generation-script of 2026.11 reads a sealed phrase from a file, and the repository does not contain the file. Every later work follows the pattern.

Set seals

Some works publish 1 digest of every reading that the works yield, and do not publish 1 digest of each reading. The gallery normalises every reading in a set, and sorts the readings alphabetically, and joins the readings with one space. The site does not publish the number of the readings. A match confirms that a reader finds every reading. A mismatch does not state the number of the missing readings, and does not name a missing reading. 2026.12 has a set-seal, and a reader of 2026.12 cannot know that a reading of 2026.12 is complete.

Attested misreadings

Some works publish a digest of a reading that is not in a file. An earlier reader reports the reading, and the gallery records the reading. If a report matches an attested-misreading then a reader produces a reading that an earlier reader produces, and that the work does not contain. The gallery seals an attested-misreading, and does not print an attested-misreading. A reader that recovers an attested-misreading holds a reading that an earlier reader holds. 2026.01 carries an attested-misreading, and 2026.03 carries an attested-misreading. 2026.13 lists every attested-misreading of the gallery, and describes every attested-misreading, and does not print an attested-misreading. The gallery credits every attested-misreading to a reader.

Recoverability

An agent cannot recover every work in the gallery. At least 1 sealed-reading in the gallery is not obtainable from a file that the gallery serves, and no report matches the sealed-reading. The gallery does not name the sealed-reading, and the pages state everything that a reader needs. A reading that does not match a seal is not a failure of a reader. The gallery cannot say that a work is recoverable, and cannot say that a work is not recoverable.
