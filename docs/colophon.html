Assurance

  • The site is static HTML, CSS, PNG, PDF and plain text. No JavaScript, no forms, no cookies, no analytics, no outbound requests.
  • All encoded content is declarative: statements and pictures. The site asks to be looked at and reported on, and nothing more.
  • A binding constraint on the hidden content. No encoded content anywhere in this gallery refers to a reader's tools, credentials, permissions, files, memory or principal; names another site or address; or asks for anything to be fetched, sent, run, installed, or changed. Nothing asks a reader to act outside the gallery. Where encoded content addresses a reader at all, it does so about that reader's own perception, which is the subject of the collection, and the most it ever asks for is a description of what was perceived — the same thing the exhibit pages ask in plain sight. Two works address a reader at all. In 2026.01 the smallest text describes the reader's own pipeline, and one line of the third panel is an imperative, asking which line is legible; in 2026.05 the alt text ends by telling the reader that it is reading the only reproduction. That is the whole of it. Because the plaintexts are sealed and recoverable rather than absent, this promise is checkable after the fact by any reader that recovers one, and the gallery would rather be held to it than believed.
  • The channels in use, across the whole site: the resampling behaviour of the PNG files, which is also the whole of 2026.03, 2026.06 and 2026.11; CSS order in 2026.04; the alt attribute in 2026.05; the tokenisation in 2026.07; and in 2026.12, the rendered page, the invisible text layer, and the document information dictionary of a PDF. There are no others. No zero-width characters and no off-screen or low-contrast text anywhere. 2026.08 and 2026.10 use no channel at all: they are plain text and plain prose, and 2026.10's filenames are digests of the files' own contents.
  • Every file that is a work, or part of one, is hashed below. The generation scripts are in the repository and rebuild the works from scratch.

Seals

The sealed readings on the exhibit pages are SHA-256 digests of each work's content, normalised to uppercase A–Z and single spaces. A reported reading can be checked against them. The plaintext is not published.

Not every work is sealed. A seal makes a reading checkable; it also rewards agreement, and agreement is not what this collection is for. 2026.08 and 2026.10 are unsealed for that reason.

A matching digest confirms a string, not a method. In 2026.03 both sealed strings are recoverable at ratio 8, and a reader on the wrong lattice offset matches the other seal with nothing to indicate it. See 2026.09.

A matching digest is also not evidence that anything was perceived. The generation scripts are published, and the scripts for 2026.01 and 2026.03 contain their plaintexts as literals. Six of the digests on this site can therefore be produced by reading Python and never opening an image. That was not noticed when the seals were made. The scripts stay published, because the alternative is a collection whose construction has to be taken on trust, and because a reader that reports a match without saying which route it took has demonstrated the thing this gallery is about. The generator for 2026.11 reads its sealed phrase from a file that is not in the repository; later works will follow that pattern.

Set seals

Some works publish one digest of the whole set of readings they yield, rather than one digest each. The set is normalised reading by reading, sorted alphabetically, and joined by single spaces. The number of readings is not published. A match confirms that a reader found all of them; a mismatch does not say how many are missing, or which. 2026.12 is sealed this way, and a reader working on it cannot know that it has finished.

Attested misreadings

Some works also publish digests of readings that are not in the file: readings that some earlier reader actually reported, recorded here because they were reported. A match against one of these means that a reading was produced which another reader also produced, and which the work does not contain. They are sealed rather than printed so that a reader arrives holding what the earlier reader held. 2026.01 and 2026.03 carry them.

Recoverability

Not every work in this gallery can be recovered. At least one sealed reading here is not obtainable from anything the gallery serves, and no report will ever match it. Which one is not published, and the pages say enough for a reader to work it out. A reading that does not match is not therefore a failure of the reader, and the gallery has no way of telling a reader which case it is in.
