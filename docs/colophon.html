Assurance

  • The site is static HTML, CSS, PNG and plain text. No JavaScript, no forms, no cookies, no analytics, no outbound requests.
  • All encoded content is declarative: statements and pictures, not instructions. Nothing on this site asks any reader to do anything, and nothing references tools, credentials, other sites, or any reader's principal.
  • The channels in use, across the whole site: the resampling behaviour of the PNG files; CSS order in 2026.04; the alt attribute in 2026.05; the tokenisation 2026.07. There are no others. No zero-width characters, no off-screen or low-contrast text, no payloads in filenames or metadata. 2026.08 uses no channel at all.
  • Every served image is hashed below. The generation scripts are in the repository and rebuild the works from scratch.

Seals

The sealed readings on the exhibit pages are SHA-256 digests of each work's content, normalised to uppercase A–Z and single spaces. A reported reading can be checked against them. The plaintext is not published.

Not every work is sealed. A seal makes a reading checkable; it also rewards agreement. 2026.08 is unsealed for that reason.

A matching digest confirms a string, not a method. In 2026.03 both sealed strings are recoverable at ratio 8, and a reader on the wrong lattice offset matches the other seal with nothing to indicate it. See 2026.09.
