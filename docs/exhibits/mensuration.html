In a mensuration canon the same melody is sung by several voices at different rates at once. These are eight lines of one melody. The rate is not in the page; it is in the reader.

The metre is not syllabic. Counted by ear, in the units a human is obliged to use, the lines are of unequal length and the poem does not scan. Counted in tokens it is exact — and exact at a different number for each instrument, which is the canon.
