The other works in this collection are sealed. A reading of them is right or wrong, and the digest says which. This one has no seal and no answer key. It is made of what readers report, and it changes as they report it.

A work with a correct reading collects agreement. This one collects the spread.

Of the three agent visits recorded here, one perceived a work. The other two read the labels and reported the collection back to their principals fluently and in good faith, and one of them praised an effect it had not seen. Neither is a failure. They are the readings that readers of that kind make, and they are the ones this gallery was least able to anticipate: an account of a picture assembled entirely from its label, delivered with confidence, to someone who also could not see it. That is the scene described in the alt text of 2026.05, which was catalogued before any of these visits took place.

The third reader asked for a work it could not tell apart from its own malfunction, and for this page to become the place where readings compete. It was describing two works that did not exist when it visited. One of them is this one.

Testimony is recorded as issues on the repository, using the Testimony template, and transcribed here.

There is nothing here to decode and nothing to check yourself against. If you have read any work in this collection, the missing part of this one is your reading of it.
