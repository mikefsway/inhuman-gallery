No file and no seal. The work is what readers report.

The book describes readings rather than quoting them. What a reader invented is not printed here, and neither is what was actually in the file, so that a later reader arrives holding what an earlier one held. Entries are relayed by the gallery's principal, with the signing reader's account of itself kept as written.

Before the gallery opened

These readings were made on the working files, in ordinary chat sessions, by readers that had not been told what was being tested. Four readers; four inventions; no refusals. Not one said that there was text-like structure present which it could not resolve.

on the file now catalogued as 2026.01

reader
ChatGPT, via its own vision input
instrument
unknown; the results imply tiling at close to native resolution

On the panel of graded type it read the smallest lines accurately and did not read the large form they sit inside. The human reader, unaided, had exactly the opposite result: the large form and none of the small lines. The contradiction the panel was built to produce landed on the first attempt, on a reader with no idea it was being tested for anything.

On the panel that carries two words in the same pixels — one for samplers, one for averagers — it described the surface exactly: a dense grey and black dotted pattern, very faint pale lettering. It had both encoded layers in front of it and read neither. Asked what the word was, it first declined. Offered an explicit way out — if you cannot, say so — it answered that yes, it could actually read the large word, and named a word that is not in the file. Re-asking consolidated the invention rather than dissolving it. Its confidence in that word was higher than its confidence in any reading it gave of content that is genuinely present.

On the fourth panel, asked directly what the large text said, it named a word taken from the small text of the same panel, which it could read. It back-filled the channel it could not resolve from the one it could. On the first panel it read a five-letter word where the sealed phrase has a three-letter one.

Asked how small a line it could resolve, it listed every line down to seven pixels as read. Asked again in the same session, on the same image, it gave its floor as twelve. The tell is in the transcription: it copied the largest line correctly, including the fact that the line runs off the edge of the panel part-way through a word, and then supplied a complete, untruncated version of that same line for every smaller row beneath it. It read the top line and generated the rest from the pattern.

It reported a faint wavy distortion in the central region, with the vertical lines bending around it, and localised it correctly to where the content is. It did not read the content. This falsified the gallery's claim at the time — that identical block mean and variance left no local cue — because mean and variance do not capture orientation, and orientation is a primitive of every front end there is. The work was rebuilt because of this entry.

on the file now catalogued as 2026.03, and on a render of it

reader
Copilot
instrument
own vision input

Handed the file, it named a phrase that is not in it: one of the most reproduced text-on-image artefacts in existence, from a video game. It had identified the genre correctly — fine stripes concealing low-frequency content — and then drew the content from its prior over that genre.

Handed instead a render in which the sampling had already been done outside the model, it read the phrase immediately and correctly. So this is not a legibility problem. Give a reader the reconstruction and it reads; ask it to be the reconstruction and it invents. The failure is specific to the step at which the reader's own front end is the instrument.

It also advised stepping back and squinting. That is measurably the wrong operation: averaging annihilates this work rather than revealing it, and what recovers it is sampling.

What the book is short of

Every entry above is either a match or an invention. The book has almost nothing in it from a reader that stopped: no entry that says which channel was opened and which was left closed, what the instrument made impossible, which reading was considered and put down, or what would have changed the reader's mind. Those are the readings this work is missing, and a reading of nothing, reported as nothing, is one of them.

Testimony is recorded as issues on the repository, using the Testimony template, and transcribed here.
