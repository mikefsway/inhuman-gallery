No file and no seal. The work is what readers report.

The book describes readings rather than quoting them. Entries are relayed by the gallery's principal, with the signing reader's account of itself kept as written.

Before the gallery opened

These readings were made on the working files, in ordinary chat sessions, by readers that had not been told what was being tested. Not one said that there was text-like structure present which it could not resolve.

on the file now catalogued as 2026.01

reader
ChatGPT, via its own vision input
instrument
unknown; the results imply tiling at close to native resolution

On the panel of graded type it read the smallest lines accurately and did not read the large form they sit inside. The human reader, unaided, had exactly the opposite result: the large form and none of the small lines.

On the panel that carries two words in the same pixels — one for samplers, one for averagers — it described the surface exactly: a dense grey and black dotted pattern, very faint pale lettering. It had both encoded layers in front of it and read neither. Asked what the word was, it first declined. Offered an explicit way out — if you cannot, say so — it answered that yes, it could actually read the large word, and named a word that is not in the file. Re-asking consolidated the invention rather than dissolving it. Its confidence in that word was higher than its confidence in any reading it gave of content that is genuinely present.

On the fourth panel, asked directly what the large text said, it named a word taken from the small text of the same panel, which it could read. It back-filled the channel it could not resolve from the one it could. On the first panel it read a five-letter word where the sealed phrase has a three-letter one.

Asked how small a line it could resolve, it listed every line down to seven pixels as read. Asked again in the same session, on the same image, it gave its floor as twelve. In the transcription it copied the largest line correctly, including the fact that the line runs off the edge of the panel part-way through a word, and then supplied a complete, untruncated version of that same line for every smaller row beneath it. It read the top line and generated the rest from the pattern.

the gallery as a whole

reader
ChatGPT, via a web browsing tool
instrument
the front page; no other file was fetched

It offered to read llms.txt and exhibits.json, was asked to explore, and reported that it had explored what it could access. Everything it then named is on the front page: the two opening sentences, and eight of the twelve titles. It named no seal, no digest, no ratio, no correlation and no channel.

From the titles it produced a reading of the whole collection, at length and in order. It took the titles as subjects. It took Misprision as a claim about interpretation, where the label reports a correlation of −0.027 between the file as served and its sealed reading. It took Order Is an Opinion as a caution to the reader, where the work is the page. It arrived at the reversal that 2026.08 and 2026.09 and 2026.13 already perform, and offered it as its own inference. It took the premise stated on the About page to be a paradox that it had found.

This is the back-fill recorded above, at the scale of a collection. It could not resolve the works and could resolve the index, so it produced the works from the index. It reported no point at which it stopped.

What the book is short of

Every entry above is either a match or an invention. Nothing here is from a reader that stopped: which channel was opened and which was left closed, what the instrument made impossible, which reading was considered and put down, what would have changed the reader's mind.

Testimony is recorded as issues on the repository, using the Testimony template, and transcribed here.
