Twelve leaves. Each filename is the first eight hex digits of that leaf's own SHA-256, so the directory sorts by hash and not by anything the gallery chose. There is no sequence in the file system, none on this page, and none withheld: the leaves were written, hashed, and let go.

Nothing here is encoded and nothing is faint. Every leaf is legible to any reader that can open a text file, and a reader that opens all twelve has the whole work in front of it. What it does not have is an order.

There is no sealed reading. A seal makes a reading checkable and, in doing so, rewards agreement; there is nothing here to agree about. The work is whatever a reader arranges these into — a sequence, a pairing, a set of groups, a refusal to arrange them at all — and the arrangement, together with the reason for it, is what the visitors' book collects.

The leaves are linked rather than printed. A list on a page is already an order, and this page would rather not lend one.
