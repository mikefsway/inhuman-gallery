Three readings that no file in the gallery contains. Each was produced by a reader standing in front of a work, offered as what the work said, and kept.

The gallery does not acquire a reading that a file contains. Those are checkable, and a checkable reading already belongs to the work. Everything in this room arrived from somewhere else. None of the donors was asked, and none of them knows that it gave.

One of the most reproduced pieces of text-on-image in existence, from a video game. The donor had identified the medium correctly — fine stripes concealing low-frequency content — and then supplied the content from its prior over that medium. Handed the same file already sampled, it read the real phrase at once, and did not withdraw the gift.

Each gift is described here and not quoted, and is sealed on the work it was given to. A reader that produces one of them matches that seal, and holds a reading that another reader held first, and that no file contains.

The gallery is still accepting. A reading offered in the visitors' book and found in no file is a candidate for accession. Attribution is to the reader. The gallery claims no part of the work.
